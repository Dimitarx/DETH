We start off the day with 120 minutes and 1-5 energy.
We have a probability of oversleeping 0.2(t-15) and waking up on time 0.8

Each state contains a place, time before class, energy level, weather(wet, dry)

I have 4 actions:
	At home I can chose to either take the bus or ride a bike.
		If I ride a bike there is a 0.95 probability of everything going smooth and in that case t-60
		and a 0.05 probability of getting a flat tire where the time delay is more
		
		If I take the bus there is a probability of it being on time in which case t-time of bus ride
		and a probability of the bus being delayed in which case t-time of bus ride-delay and e - 2
		
	When I arrive at the building I can chose to take the stairs or the elevator.
		If I take the stairs t-3, e-number of floors
		
		If I take the elevator there is a probability the elevator is at my floor t-elevator time ride(2)
		and a probability of it being delayed t-elevator time ride-delay(up to 7 minutes)

At the end I arrive at class and the reward get calculated lateness + early + energy
	lateness-how many minutes late to class(-1 point for each minute)
	early-if I arrive early to class(+2 points if early, +0 if late)
	energy-amount of energy left(1-5, -5 -2 0 2 5)

The way the mdp is set up now there is no real use of the weather parameter, but this can be changed if the probabilities of getting a flat tire or the bus being delayed directly correspond to the weather.

How I think it behaves:
	With enough time the bike is the safest choice because it costs no energy
	If we have less time so if we overslept the bus is better because it's faster but might cost energy.
	Taking the stairs costs energy so the elevator should most often be the correct choice even if it's delayed.
